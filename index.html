Guide
how the program works

Double progression

Keep the same load until every working set reaches the top of its rep range with clean technique and about 1–2 reps in reserve. Then add the smallest practical weight and rebuild the reps from the bottom of the range.

Example — chest-supported row. Baseline is 22.5 kg/side × 7,7. Work toward 3×6–10: 7/7/6 → 8/7/7 → 9/8/8 → 10/10/10 — then add weight.

Rest & effort

Rest: 2–3 min for heavy compounds, 90–120 sec for accessories. Take longer if performance would otherwise drop.

Failure: compounds generally stop at 1–2 RIR. Isolation work can occasionally approach failure on the final set.

These build the broad, muscular look in clothes. Legs and core stay fully trained for strength and proportion.

Nutrition & supplements

Whey: Nakpro Impact Whey to help reach protein needs.

Creatine: 5 g every day — timing isn't critical.

Calories: aim for gradual muscle gain, not an aggressive bulk. A mass gainer isn't required.

Hydration: ~3 L/day baseline, adjusted for heat and sweating.

Return & recovery

Week 1: ~70–80% of normal working effort, avoid failure.

Week 2 on: work toward the listed RIR targets. Don't try to regain three months of performance in one week — if fatigue builds up, cut accessory sets first.

Safety

Warm up before heavy work. Use a spotter or safeties when appropriate. Learn RDL technique with light loads first. Stop for sharp or unusual pain — consistency, overload and recovery matter more than soreness.
